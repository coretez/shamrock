A fork is welcome — the license permits it. It needs its own name and its own
mark.

## Why this exists

A source license can be worked around, and every release here converts to
Apache 2.0 after two years. The name and the mark do not expire. For a
source-available project with a commercial path, the trademark is the durable
asset — it is what keeps "Shamrock" meaning *this* software.

## Registration note

The word "shamrock" is common, which makes the bare word difficult to register
for software. The stronger, registrable asset is the **compound mark** — the
wordmark set in its own treatment together with the clover logo. Use the two
together in official materials so the compound identity accrues distinctiveness
from the start.
